# Questions without answer text: distribution check (2026-10-18)

After OCR (140 scanned answers recovered), **505 of 393,501 questions (0.128%)** still have no answer text. A further 432 records are broken on the server and are not in the corpus.

**Causes:** mostly 1999–2009 starred answers marked "statement laid on the Table" with no statement file. Also 21 Hindi-only replies, about 29 documents without a parsable ANSWER heading, and 4 corrupt files. (The one scan first rejected for a question-number mismatch turned out to be mirrored and upside-down; it was transcribed by hand and recovered.) The table counts were computed before that recovery: one LS16 starred opposition-asked question less is missing.

**Bounds:** the opposition-government difference in missingness is 0.03 percentage points. Suppose every missing answer were coded either as fully responsive (score 1) or as wholly unresponsive (score 0). The H1 contrast could then move by at most ~0.001 on the 0–1 scale. The LS17–LS18 contrast (H2/H4) could move by at most ~0.001. Missing answers cannot account for estimated differences of any substantive size. They are reported as exclusions, not imputed.
