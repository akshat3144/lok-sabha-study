## What holds (exploratory; every analysis run is reported above, none dropped)

**RQ4**
- Responsiveness differs strongly by ministry: scores run from 0.748 (Law and Justice) to 0.943 (Atomic Energy) around an overall 0.836; 47 of 55 ministry CIs exclude the overall mean. The ranking is stable to LS composition (Spearman 0.94, raw vs LS-adjusted). The top is scientific/technical ministries (Atomic Energy, Earth Sciences, Science and Technology, New and Renewable Energy, Power); the bottom is Law and Justice, Labour and Employment, Minority Affairs, Corporate Affairs, Personnel.
- Security is not uniformly low: External Affairs #26 of 55, Defence #38, Home Affairs #46. Pooled, security scores 0.815 vs 0.837 for other ministries (loss gap +0.022, CI [+0.019, +0.025]); the gap is carried by Home Affairs (loss +0.038 vs other ministries) and Defence (+0.016); External Affairs is -0.008 (better than average).
- JURISDICTION is concentrated in State/Concurrent-list ministries: Tier A (health, agriculture, home, water, education) 2.07% of parts, Tier B 1.59%, Tier C (Union/other) 0.52%; Tier A holds 44% of all JURISDICTION parts with 24% of parts. It is uneven within the tier: highest in AYUSH, Housing and Urban Affairs, Panchayati Raj, Water/Jal Shakti, Home Affairs; low in Agriculture (about 1%) and near zero in Defence and External Affairs. The mapping is by ministry name only and approximate; the topic-cluster check points the same way (health, water, education and courts topics highest).
- JURISDICTION deflection rises over time: all ministries 0.71% of parts (LS13) to 1.83% (LS18); Tier A 1.34% to 3.06%, Tier B 0.79% to 2.70%, Tier C 0.47% to 0.72%. Among non-FULL parts its share rises from 3.5% (LS13) to 5-6% (LS16-18). Home Affairs peaks in LS17 (5.9% of parts); Water is highest in LS13 (4.7%) and dips to 2.5% in LS15.
- The security gap is NOT mainly WITHHELD. WITHHELD is where security is most distinctive (3.4x the rate of other ministries; 24% of the loss gap) but it is under 1% of parts. GENERIC (34%), NOT_MAINTAINED (22%), DEFERRAL (21%) and JURISDICTION (19%) carry most of it; security answers less often give a different question's answer (-12%, an offset). By ministry: Home Affairs = NOT_MAINTAINED + JURISDICTION (crime and State data); Defence = WITHHELD + DEFERRAL + GENERIC; External Affairs = GENERIC only, offset elsewhere.

**RQ1**
- Embedding cosine barely separates answers from non-answers: AUC(FULL vs any non-FULL) = 0.541 [0.509, 0.575] (design-weighted 0.530). It separates NONE-labelled parts moderately (0.640) but PARTIAL parts score no lower than FULL (0.474).
- The failure is type-specific. NOT_MAINTAINED non-answers have HIGHER cosine than FULL answers (AUC 0.378 [0.272, 0.481]; median 0.615 vs 0.523 for FULL); JURISDICTION is a coin flip (0.501 [0.417, 0.598]); INCOMPLETE (0.480) and DIFFERENT_QUESTION (0.578) are weak. Only DEFERRAL (0.708), DOES_NOT_ARISE (0.760), OTHER and WITHHELD (n = 6, unreliable) are separable. The untuned base model shows the same pattern.
- Second model: gemini-3.1-flash-lite agrees materially less with the human gold: kappa 0.570 [0.527, 0.612] vs 0.711 [0.672, 0.748] for 3.8-flash (difference -0.141, CI [-0.183, -0.099]); it falls below the 0.6 adequacy threshold used for model choice. Evasion-type kappa 0.602 vs 0.770. Cost $0.44.
- Leniency: design-weighted part-level mean score gold 0.797; 3.8-flash 0.825 (bias +0.027, CI [+0.012, +0.043]: slightly lenient); 3.1-flash-lite 0.787 (bias -0.011, CI [-0.030, +0.007]: not distinguishable from zero). The lite model's near-zero net bias hides larger two-way noise (142 gold FULL parts downgraded to PARTIAL vs 47 for 3.8, plus upgrades of PARTIAL to FULL).
- LS ordering on the gold questions is similar but not identical: LS18 is lowest under gold and both models and LS13 or LS14 highest; the full six-LS ordering is not identical between the two models (Kendall tau 0.73, bootstrap CI [0.07, 1.00]; gold vs 3.8 0.87; gold vs lite 0.60). With about 65 questions per LS only the broad pattern (LS18 lowest) is resolvable.

**Caveats**: the ministry-level jurisdiction mapping is approximate; ministry gaps are descriptive (no controls for topic, asker bloc or LS beyond the indirect LS standardisation); WITHHELD has few cases per ministry (zero-count cells give degenerate CIs of [0, 0]); gold evasion-type cells are small (WITHHELD n = 6, DOES_NOT_ARISE n = 13, DEFERRAL n = 20); B5 uses 400 questions so LS-level comparisons are noisy; LS18 is a partial parliament.
